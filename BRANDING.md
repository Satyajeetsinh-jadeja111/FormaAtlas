# Forma Atlas

Public frontend identity: Forma Atlas, by SR Infotech.
Tagline: Your command center for intelligent work.

The existing backend/application identifier remains Forma so stored integrations and credentials continue using their existing names.

## Assets

Vector assets live under public/brand. atlas.png is the current product mark and favicon. atlas.svg is a retired previous mark. LinkedIn and YouTube marks identify their respective integration workspaces; they do not imply endorsement.

## Future agents

public/app.js defines agentLogo. Its explicit service registry currently maps linkedin and youtube. An agent can supply a service field or use its stable id. Unknown services render workflow.svg, so new agents always have a consistent icon without fetching an untrusted image URL. Add reviewed local SVG assets to this registry and the server.py static-file allowlist when adding another service. Do not assign another service's logo to an unrelated agent.

Shared tokens and responsive component styles are in the Forma Atlas section of public/style.css. Agent visibility and access remain controlled by the server.
